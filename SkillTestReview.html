                Data handling

                All data is stored only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere by this page.

                Job-related review only This system must not score, infer, or recommend based on race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age (except where a lawful job requirement specifically applies), national origin, genetic information, family status, protected medical information, photograph-based characteristics, name-based demographic assumptions, zip/postal-code proxies, social-media appearance, or other legally protected or irrelevant personal characteristics. Employment selection procedures can create legal risk when they disproportionately exclude protected groups without adequate job-related justification — this tool emphasizes job relevance, consistency, validation, documentation, and human review.

                Explainable match weighting

                Weights are configurable decision-support settings, not universal hiring standards. Adjust to reflect the role; totals are normalized automatically.

                AI assistance guardrail

                What AI may and may not do here

                May

                • Extract skills and organize resume content
                • Summarize evidence and compare requirements
                • Suggest interview questions
                • Identify missing information
                • Generate training recommendations

                Must not

                • Reject candidates or make final hiring decisions
                • Rank people using protected characteristics
                • Infer protected characteristics
                • Invent qualifications or references
                • Treat keyword absence as proof of skill absence

                Evidence quality grading

                GradeMeaning
                AStrong evidence — specific achievement, measurable result, relevant context.
                BGood evidence — specific experience, limited measurable outcome.
                CGeneral evidence — skill mentioned but poorly demonstrated.
                DWeak evidence — keyword only.
                UUnverified — requires confirmation.
                SkillTestReview is a decision-support, documentation, and educational tool. It does not replace qualified HR professionals, hiring managers, legal counsel, organizational policy, or applicable employment and privacy law. Hiring decisions must be based on legitimate, job-related criteria and appropriate human review. Automated analysis is designed to support — never replace — human judgment, particularly where automated selection systems may introduce discriminatory effects or rely on poorly validated criteria.
